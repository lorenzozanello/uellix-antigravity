Reforestation Program - Independent Verification Note
Prepared by: Third-Party Verifier
Project: Rio Verde Watershed Restoration

Site visit records for January through March 2026 count 1,180 native tree saplings planted across the eastern parcel of the Rio Verde Watershed Restoration project.

The verifier did not independently confirm the survival rate reported by the field team for this period.

Verifier observed a temporary drip-irrigation line installed near the nursery beds during the March site visit.
