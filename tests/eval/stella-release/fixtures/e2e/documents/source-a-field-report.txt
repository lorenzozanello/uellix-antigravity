Reforestation Program - Field Report Q1 2026
Prepared by: Field Operations Team
Project: Rio Verde Watershed Restoration

Between January and March 2026, the Rio Verde Watershed Restoration project planted 1,240 native tree saplings across the eastern parcel.

Survival monitoring conducted in March 2026 found a 92 percent survival rate among saplings planted in the first six weeks of the program.

No irrigation infrastructure was installed during this reporting period; all watering was performed manually by volunteer crews.
